Darwin's Ark Internship

This repository contains the two projects I completed for my internship at Darwin's Ark (July 13, 2026 - October 30, 2026).

The cat-coat-phenotypes project compares owner-reported coat phenotypes with phenotypes predicted from cats’ genotypes to evaluate their agreement.

The dog-size project asks whether genome-wide genetic information predicts dog height beyond what breed admixture already tells us. In held-out dogs, a polygenic score (PGS) explained modestly more height variance than admixture, and about four-fifths of its ancestry-independent signal came from known size loci.
